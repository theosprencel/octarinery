# What Is the Notes Folder

The **Notes** folder is a place to store ideas, thoughts, happenings, tasks, questions and any other information that shouldn’t be forgotten. **Notes** (under this definition) arise from daily activities, as opposed to being purposely invoked as part of a project or research question.

## How is the Notes folder different from the others?

The **Notes** folder differs from the others as follows:

- **Inbox** – Where **Inbox** contents are raw and not definitively assigned to anything, **Notes** contents are categorizable and have been or are currently being processed.
- **Projects** – **Notes** contents aren’t part of a single, defined project; rather, they arise from day-to-day life, work and activities. Notes that belong to a specific project should be filed with that project under the **Projects** folder.
- **Documentation** – Whereas **Notes** are shorter bits of information, **Documentation** is the fully matured, expressed and applied version of notes, having been thoroughly reviewed and refined to provide the best possible reference on a specific process, topic or entity. **Notes**, by contrast, aren’t as refined and don’t necessarily ever need to be.
