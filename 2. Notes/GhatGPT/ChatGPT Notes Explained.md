## What are ChatGPT Notes?

ChatGPT notes fall into one of two categories:

1. Advice from ChatGPT about ChatGPT.
2. Advice from ChatGPT about anything else.

While there are plenty of other ways to classify said notes, this is the one that will be true of all ChatGPT notes.
